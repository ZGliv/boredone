# Bordone: explore Pittsburgh, keep the memories

Product direction confirmed by Zack, November 3, 2026. This document is the handoff for future builders, including another model. Read the existing code and project plan before implementation. This supersedes the October 31 priority of proving a general city engine in Columbus: Pittsburgh and its surrounding regions come first. The underlying system can eventually support more cities, but do not build a city marketplace or global scraper now.

## The product

A personal, photo-first exploration map of the Pittsburgh region. Find a worthwhile outing, go with someone, keep photos of the visit, and watch a map of your life fill in. The map is the home screen; photos and actual places are the content. Preserve the comfortable custom map, dark mode, fast cached photo markers, calm selection movement, and offline saves.

Pittsburgh means the lived metropolitan region, not just municipal boundaries. Zack lives around 15317; the South Hills, McMurray, Canonsburg and Southpointe belong in the core experience. Expand outward with useful, verified places rather than filling the map with every business. Current coverage is partial and concentrated along the existing corridor. Do not label the current catalog complete Pittsburgh coverage.

## Geographic model

Use three levels: Pittsburgh region → exploration areas → places. Exploration areas are human-recognizable neighborhoods or nearby towns, each with a stable ID and a verified polygon. Broader collections such as South Hills, North Hills, East End and river towns can group areas, but must not double-count them. Proposed names are not an approved boundary dataset.

Initial geographic work: inventory current venue coordinates; source reusable neighborhood/municipal boundaries; review a manageable set of areas with Zack. Start with the existing corridor before expanding north/east/west. Split Pittsburgh neighborhoods and surrounding municipalities at a useful scale; avoid comparing a tiny neighborhood directly with an enormous county. Assign each place one primary exploration area. Preserve unmatched/boundary cases for review rather than guessing. Record source/license/version for boundaries.

## The main interaction

1. Open Pittsburgh with nothing selected. See places, with optional area outlines.
2. Filter by outing type and exploration status. Choose a nearby area with something worth doing.
3. Save for Tonight or make a lasting plan.
4. After the outing, save a visit: select photos, confirm date, optionally add time, companion and notes. Notes and photos must never be mandatory for recording a real visit.
5. The visited place and its area update. Reopen the album later from the map or timeline.

Keep exploration filters separate from outing categories: All areas / Not yet visited / Visited / In progress / Completed challenge. These combine with Food, Bars, Coffee, Entertainment, Games & creative, Sports. Offer “Hide places I’ve visited” independently: revisiting a favorite is still valuable.

## What it means to complete a city

Do not equate one visit with fully exploring an area. Do not make users visit every restaurant. Businesses open and close; that denominator would punish users and never finish.

Use two honest layers:
- **My footprint:** an area becomes “Visited” after one user-confirmed visit. It answers where you have been. A subtle fill/outline shows this, with a legend and accessible list alternative. No background location tracking or automatic visit claims.
- **Exploration challenges:** finite, versioned collections of worthwhile experiences in an area. Example proposal: try a food place, do an activity, and make another visit on a different day. Treat this as a design example, not a shipped rule. Adapt challenges to what genuinely exists locally; no impossible sports quota in an area without sports outings.

“Complete Pittsburgh” means finish a named edition of the Pittsburgh exploration collection, not literally see everything. Show exact progress: “8 of 24 areas visited” or “South Hills collection: 4 of 7 challenges.” Counts must use a defined, versioned set. Retain earned completion when venues close or a new edition adds areas. New editions invite further exploration; they never erase an earned achievement. Never imply that photo counts prove visits or award repeat progress for uploading duplicate photos.

Prefer a satisfying map reveal, small milestones and a personal annual recap over XP, leaderboards, streak pressure, confetti on every action, or extra dashboard screens. Public/social sharing is optional later. User visits and companions are private by default.

## Memories are the foundation

A place has a stable identity; each visit has a separate identity. Repeated dates at one restaurant produce separate albums. A visit stores place ID, local visit date, optional time/timezone, companions, optional notes, photos and chosen cover. Allow custom places and historical visits. Corrections/deletions recompute footprint honestly; retain earned challenge policy explicitly rather than silently choosing it.

Existing outing IDs, saves, photos, IndexedDB/local storage and backup formats must migrate without loss. Never overwrite existing records based on name matching. Separate photo thumbnails/previews from originals; show what is stored and backed up. Cloud sync is a later explicit slice with private access controls, recoverable uploads and clear pending/synced states. Browser-local compressed photos are not a guaranteed archival backup. Keep the $0 prototype budget; no paid services without approval.

## Data and recommendation engine

Build repeatable Pittsburgh imports/enrichment and place identity reconciliation, not hand edits forever. Store provider IDs, canonical ID, coordinates, primary area, categories, website, source/freshness, closure status and photo provenance separately from personal visits. Suppressing a closed discovery listing must never delete memories. Deduplicate branches with location evidence, not just names.

Prioritize independent food/drink, entertainment, active outings and creative experiences. Add parks/trails only when deliberately curated as worthwhile outings; do not restore generic civic POIs. Show unknown opening hours honestly. Do not invent ratings or recommendation evidence. Website photos are not automatically licensed merely because they are publicly reachable. Keep attribution accessible without floating credits banners on the map.

Rank using actual interests, category variety, distance, personal feedback and unvisited-area opportunities. “Try somewhere new in an area you haven’t explored” is the distinctive recommendation. Do not silently replace good choices with far-away places solely to chase completion.

Cache bounded regional data and optimized images. Filter locally. Do not fetch individual venue photos on every pan/zoom, recreate marker DOM during camera movement, or download the entire region’s map tiles automatically. Preserve the explicit offline-download limits and graceful list fallback.
